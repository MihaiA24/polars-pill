Benchmark results

Tables separate cold, warm and legacy measurements. Warm rows show the median and min–max within one benchmark batch; raw attempts remain in JSONL. Cold charts include library/session startup; warm and legacy charts use query time. Cold does not mean a flushed filesystem cache. Adjacent bars compare versions; whiskers show min–max. Failed or incomplete groups have no bar. Legacy attempts retain their original timer scopes and are not pooled with the new protocol.
